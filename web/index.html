Settings & About

Download cookies, download server, privacy and app information

YouTube / Facebook cookies

If YouTube says “Sign in to confirm you're not a bot”, or a Facebook/Instagram video is private or login-only, the downloader needs your login cookies for that site. They stay on this PC (saved in the app's data folder).

Cookie source

Desktop app only.

Browser

Close the browser first; Chrome/Edge/Avast on Windows often lock or encrypt cookies, so cookies.txt is the most reliable.

Download server (advanced)

Mobile app: leave empty to use the built-in LAS CONVERT server. Or enter your own, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:3000.

cookies.txt

Install the “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY” extension, open the site (youtube.com, facebook.com, instagram.com…) while signed in, click the extension → Export, then load the file here.

About LAS CONVERT

LAS CONVERT v1.8.0 — convert, compress and download video, audio, images and subtitles.

Privacy

Files you convert, compress, edit or transcribe are processed on this device and are not uploaded. Online downloads (YouTube, Facebook, …) are fetched by the download server: on the desktop app that runs locally on this PC; on the mobile app it is the LAS CONVERT download server, which receives the link you paste and deletes the file after sending it to you. Cookies you add stay in this app's data folder.
